DTEW Cord v0.3.0 brings back the ready-made themes and shows who is in a DM call before you join.

> DTEW Cord is not endorsed by Discord. Signing in a normal account with an unofficial client may break Discord's Terms of Service and can lead to account enforcement.

### New
- Nine ready-made themes are back: Forest, Latte, Midnight, Ocean, Rose, Golden, Obsidian, Teal and Black, with the same colors as before. They're in Settings › Appearance after the built-in themes. Hover a swatch to see who made it, and use Edit to make your own copy.
- When a DM or group DM has a call going that you haven't joined, the conversation now shows who is in it: each person's avatar and name, a +N circle when the window is too narrow for everyone, and a Join call button.

### Not brought back
- Forest Piano and Soft White, which were mostly background images. Themes don't support images yet.
- The font size and spacing tweaks some of the old themes had.

### Download
- Windows: dtewcord-v0.3.0-Windows-X64.zip. Extract it and run dtewcord.exe; run install-notifications.ps1 once for system notifications. Earlier installs update themselves from this repository's releases.

### Not included yet
- The Slint client in apps/slint is not packaged in this release.

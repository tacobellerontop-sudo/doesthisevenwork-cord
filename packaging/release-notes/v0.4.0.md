DTEW Cord v0.4.0 makes streams lighter on your CPU, tidies Settings, and shows your own status properly.

> DTEW Cord is not endorsed by Discord. Signing in a normal account with an unofficial client may break Discord's Terms of Service and can lead to account enforcement.

### New
- Your own status: the status and custom status you pick in the account menu now show in group DM member lists and DM rows, instead of you appearing offline. Invisible shows as offline, as on Discord.
- Offline people have greyscale avatars and darker names. The colour comes back while you hover the row.
- The area above a DM call you haven't joined is no longer black. The conversation shows through a light frosted haze.
- Settings are grouped into User settings (your Discord account), App settings (how DTEW Cord looks and behaves) and System settings (this computer). The GPU options moved from General to a new Graphics page.

### Faster
- Screen sharing uses much less CPU. Scaling and colour conversion now happen on the GPU, and the local preview is smaller and refreshes less often. If the GPU path doesn't work on your graphics driver, sharing falls back to the CPU automatically.
- Watching streams copies and allocates less per frame, and the window only repaints when a new picture is due.
- Video attachments are converted and rotated on the GPU instead of the CPU.

### Known issues
- The GPU path for screen sharing hasn't been tried on many graphics drivers yet. If shared colours look wrong, please open an issue.

### Download
- Windows: dtewcord-v0.4.0-Windows-X64.zip. Extract it and run dtewcord.exe; run install-notifications.ps1 once for system notifications. Earlier installs update themselves from this repository's releases.
